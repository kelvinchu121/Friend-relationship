Aristotle’s Takeaway

Utility (exam notes) and Pleasure (late-night hotpot) bring convenience and joy, while Virtuous bonds sustain us through hard nights.

Behavioral & Emotional Buffer

Supportive peers reinforce sleep, hydration, and self-worth—lowering anxiety before stress spirals into burnout.

Biological Protection

Sharing burdens downregulates norepinephrine, protects cortisol-sensitive genes from chronic inflammation, and strengthens immunity.
